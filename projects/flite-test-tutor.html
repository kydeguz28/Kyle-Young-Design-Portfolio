Project 08 / RC aviation build

Flite Test Tutor

A trainer aircraft build using Flite Test's Power Pack B, focused on clean integration, balance, serviceability, repairability, and preflight discipline.

Role
Build, electronics integration, setup checks
Platform
Flite Test Tutor foamboard trainer
Electronics
Power Pack B, motor, ESC, servos, receiver
Focus
Center of gravity, control throws, repair access

Challenge

A kit becomes a working aircraft system.

The project was not about inventing a new aircraft. The engineering work was making the kit build clean, balanced, serviceable, and ready for real flights with people learning alongside Kyle.

Contribution

Integration details matter.

Kyle laid out the motor and prop at the nose, routed the ESC and receiver inside the fuselage, installed servos for predictable control throws, kept the battery accessible for balance adjustments, and checked wiring clearance around moving parts.

01
Airframe build

Fold foamboard parts, glue joints, spars, skewers, rubber bands, and alignment checks.

02
Electronics integration

Install propulsion, control surfaces, receiver, wiring, and battery placement.

03
Preflight discipline

Check center of gravity, control direction, wing mounting, and repair access.

Evidence

Build and completion.

The available photos show the plane during foamboard construction and as a completed trainer setup.

Reflection

Small aircraft punish sloppy assumptions.

The project reinforced careful inspection, fast repair thinking, and the way small alignment, linkage, or weight-placement errors can show up immediately in physical testing.
