Current work

From base yaw to tool roll.

I am developing a six-degree-of-freedom robotic arm with base yaw, shoulder pitch, elbow pitch, wrist roll, wrist pitch, and tool roll. Current work focuses on how joint geometry, motor packaging, and reduction choices affect the overall mechanism.

The wrist-pitch concept uses a NEMA 11 motor with a 5:1 belt reduction. The remaining transmission choices are provisional while I work through packaging and torque requirements.

My earlier differential wrist prototype documents the preceding hands-on mechanism work. The full six-axis arm is an ongoing project, with assembly and measured performance still to come.

Interactive concept

Explore all six joints.

The browser-based explorer lets you orbit the arm, move J1–J6, edit reductions, and inspect motor rotation relative to joint motion. Its dimensions and motor blocks are schematic; it is a design aid rather than validated CAD or a hardware controller.
